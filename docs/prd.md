## Simple App Plan (Non-Technical)

### App Goal

**Inspiro** is a lightweight app that gives users quick daily inspiration through short motivational quotes.
No advice. No tracking emotions. No promises. Just words that feel good.

The app exists to do **one thing well**:
👉 Show a quote when the user asks for it.

---

## What the App Does (and Does NOT Do)

### It DOES:

* Show motivational quotes
* Let users tap **“Inspire Me”**
* Optionally rephrase a quote (shorter / funnier)
* Let users share quotes

### It DOES NOT:

* Diagnose, treat, or improve mental health
* Give life advice
* Track mood, habits, or behavior
* Collect personal data

This keeps it Play Store safe ✅

---

## Core User Story (Main Flow)

> As a user, I open the app, tap **“Inspire Me”**, read a quote, and either smile, share it, or get another one.

That’s it. No onboarding complexity.
